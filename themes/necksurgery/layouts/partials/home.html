Meet Dr Matt
Stretton

Dr Matt is a New Zealand-trained Otolaryngologist (Ear, Nose and Throat Surgeon) with specialist expertise in Head and Neck, Thyroid, Paediatric and Sinus and Nasal conditions.

After graduating from the University of Otago, Matt completed his surgical training throughout Auckland and Northland, before being awarded Fellowship of the Royal Australasian College of Surgeons.

Matt is a Consultant ENT Surgeon at North Shore Hospital and regularly participates in multidisciplinary oncology meetings at Auckland Hospital. He also contributes to the regional after-hours on-call service at Auckland Hospital.

To further develop his subspecialty expertise, Matt completed an Advanced Fellowship at the Royal Adelaide Hospital in South Australia. This highly regarded fellowship provided advanced training in Head and Neck, Thyroid, Reconstructive and Robotic Surgery.

Matt is committed to providing thorough, considered and personalised care. He believes in taking the time to understand each patient’s concerns, explaining their diagnosis and treatment options clearly, and working together to determine the most appropriate approach to treatment.
